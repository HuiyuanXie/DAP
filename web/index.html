Devolved Agent Parliament

A small society that investigates incidents, makes laws, and lives with the consequences.

Local prototype
BETA · Under active development. This is an early research prototype. Behaviour and features may change as we learn.

Why four rounds? The default shows a privacy problem twice, then a resource conflict twice, so you can observe how the society responds when each situation recurs. Choose 1–10 rounds; four is a short demonstration, not an established experiment length.

What does the seed do? It only selects fictional record identifiers such as P-17 or P-29. The same seed gives the same identifiers. It does not change scenario order, randomise agent decisions, or make live model responses reproducible.

Demo decisions are scripted to demonstrate the mechanism; they are not experimental results.
